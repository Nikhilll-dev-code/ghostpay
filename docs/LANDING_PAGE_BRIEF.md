# Landing page brief

Audience: hackathon judges and developers evaluating an AI agent that touches payments.

Hero headline: Automate refunds and discounts without giving AI the keys to your money.

Subheadline: Ghost reads each customer request and proposes an action. A deterministic Trust Engine checks it against your Authority Passport before anything reaches PayPal Sandbox.

Primary call to action: Open the control room (links to the sign in page).

Sections in order: hero with an example decision, the problem, a three line tagline reveal, five benefits, three step how it works, proof (the ten Ghostbreaker cases with expected results), FAQ with eight questions, final call to action, footer with privacy and terms.

Proof rules: no testimonials, logos or usage numbers are shown because none exist. Proof is limited to facts from this repository. The hero decision card is labeled illustrative and mirrors the seeded demo scenario that is blocked with DISCOUNT_EXCEEDS_LIMIT.

SEO: title, meta description, Open Graph and Twitter tags, FAQPage structured data, one h1.
